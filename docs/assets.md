# Third-party assets

One line per binary asset that is not Iridesium's own: what it is, where it
came from, who made it, under which licence, and where it lives in this
repository. Nothing yet: every texture is Iridesium's — drawn by hand (the
names in `HAND_DRAWN` in `tools/make_textures.py`) or by that script — every
sound is Iridesium's, synthesised by `tools/make_sounds.py`, and the
template's block texture and sound are gone. The campfire model is
Iridesium's too, modelled by hand: `art/campfire.glb` is the source, and
`tools/make_campfire.py` builds `models/campfire_lit.glb`,
`models/campfire_unlit.glb` and their palette `models/campfire.png` from it.
The stations' placeholder models and their palette `models/stations.png`
are drawn by `tools/make_models.py`.
